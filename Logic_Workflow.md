# AI Analysis Workflow 🧠⚙️

This document describes the logical steps the AI should follow to analyze Dento-Facial harmony.

### Step 1: Image Pre-processing
* **Input:** Frontal and Profile clinical photos.
* **Action:** Detect facial landmarks (Eyes, Nose, Lips, Chin).

### Step 2: Reference Line Establishment
* **Vertical Midline:** Alignment of the face and dental midline.
* **Horizontal Planes:** Inter-pupillary line and Occlusal plane.

### Step 3: Measurement Calculation
* **Distance Measurement:** Calculating tooth width-to-height ratios.
* **Angular Measurement:** Calculating the Nasolabial angle (Target: 90-110 degrees).
* **Profile Assessment:** Checking the E-line (Ricketts) status (Pre-op vs. Ideal).

### Step 4: Discrepancy Reporting
* Identify the "Aesthetic Gap" (Difference between current measurement and ideal harmony).
* Suggest clinical adjustments for the treatment plan.
