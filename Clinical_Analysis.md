# Clinical Diagnostic Measurements 📊

This file outlines the key parameters used for AI-driven facial and dental analysis.

### 1. Dental Esthetics (Macro & Micro)
* **Interincisal Line:** Measuring symmetry with the facial midline.
* **Smile Arc:** Analyzing the relationship between the lower lip and maxillary incisal edges.
* **Golden Proportion:** Checking the apparent width of maxillary anterior teeth.

### 2. Facial Harmony Analysis
* **Nasolabial Angle:** Assessment of the profile and lip support.
* **Vertical Facial Thirds:** Measuring the balance between upper, middle, and lower facial segments.
* **E-Line (Ricketts' Esthetic Line):** Analyzing the projection of the lips in relation to the nose and chin.

### 3. Diagnosis Goals
* **Measurement Accuracy:** Converting clinical images into digital data.
* **Outcome Prediction:** Using analysis to visualize the best treatment result.
